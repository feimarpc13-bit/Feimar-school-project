Who is Mr. Restu?

He is Mr. Restu, a dedicated mathematics teacher at Saint Ignatius Senior High School in Singkawang. He teaches mathematics to me and other students from classes 10A to 10H. Additionally, he teaches many other tenth-grade students, as well as some students in the eleventh and twelfth grades. He is truly a remarkable teacher at our school.

Physical Appearance

Mr. Restu has a distinct physical appearance that makes him easily recognizable. He has fair skin, thinning hair, and small eyes. He doesn’t wear glasses by the way.

Personality

Mr. Restu is known for his kind personality and fun, stress-free teaching style that students love. He is a kind and humble teacher in his daily interactions with everyone. He is also a humorous person who brings joy to those around him.

Most importantly, he teaches with enthusiasm, creating an environment where students can learn mathematics without feeling stressed. Not only that, but Mr. Restu sometimes tells us jokes that make the atmosphere more relaxed and enjoyable.

These qualities are the reasons why I chose Mr. Restu as the best teacher at Saint Ignatius Senior High School in Singkawang.

Conclusion

In conclusion, Mr. Restu is an inspiring and unforgettable teacher. With his unique appearance, humorous personality, and enjoyable teaching methods, learning mathematics becomes a wonderful experience for his students.
